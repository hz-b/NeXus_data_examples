This repository collects examples of NeXus data conversion for various experimental techniques at BESSY II.

ICALEPCS 2025: In this example, we convert the experiment data from mySpot beamline of BESSY II to two NeXus application definitons (NXfluo and NXmonopd). The experiment includes the simultaneous measurement on the sample with two experimental techniques, fluorescence and X-ray diffraction, to correlate the occurrence of crystalline phases with the elemental distribution.
